# LinkedIn India Job Market Analysis

![Project banner](Image/Report_Home.png)

## Project Overview

The LinkedIn India Job Market Analysis project explores hiring trends, applicant behavior, industry demand, and geographical opportunities across India using Power BI.

The project analyzes 949 job postings from 615 companies across 228 sectors and evaluates over 111K job applications to identify meaningful patterns in the employment market.

---

## Business Problem

Understanding job market dynamics is essential for job seekers, recruiters, and organizations.

This project aims to answer key business questions such as:

* Which industries are hiring the most?
* Which cities provide the highest number of opportunities?
* What job roles are most in demand?
* Which companies attract the highest number of applicants?
* What work arrangements are most common?

## Key Insights

### Top Industries by Applicants

* IT Services and IT Consulting
* Software Development
* Technology, Information and Internet

### Most Popular Cities

* Bengaluru
* Mumbai
* Gurugram
* Pune
* Chennai

### Most Demanded Roles

* Data Scientist
* Product Manager
* Business Development Manager
* Content Writer
* Financial Analyst

### Highest Applications Received

* Data Scientist
* Product Manager

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Power BI can transform raw job market data into actionable business insights. Through interactive dashboards and data-driven analysis, the project highlights hiring patterns, industry demand, geographical opportunities, and applicant behavior across India.
