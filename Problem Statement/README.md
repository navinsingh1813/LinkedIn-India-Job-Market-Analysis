# Problem Statement

## Business Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze LinkedIn job market data and identify trends related to:

* Job posting activity
* Industry demand
* Company hiring behavior
* Applicant preferences
* Geographical opportunities
* Experience level requirements
* Work arrangements

## Key Questions

* Which industries are hiring the most?
* Which cities offer the highest job opportunities?
* Which roles receive the most applications?
* What experience levels are most demanded?
* What work arrangements are most common?

The project aims to provide actionable insights for job seekers, recruiters, and hiring organizations.
